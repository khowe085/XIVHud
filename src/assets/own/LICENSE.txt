Third-party notices for the image files in this directory.

This directory holds XIVHud's own framework art alongside the UI chrome
imported from the xivcrossbar addon. Every imported image is a verbatim,
byte-for-byte copy of its source file - no image was re-encoded, resized, or
otherwise transformed.

XIVHud's own art
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    indicator.png    the skillchain indicator's bar: a white square, tinted at
                     draw time
    panel.png        an 8x8 white square, tinted at runtime to draw the panel
                     behind the equip viewer's grid
    overlay.png      the framework's own square, tinted for layout mode's
                     highlight box

All three are XIVHud's own work. They are covered by no upstream notice, and
nothing below applies to them.

      black-square.png, red-x.png,       (bar_bg_compact.png is the panel
      frame_step1.png - frame_step8.png   behind the active side; red-x.png
