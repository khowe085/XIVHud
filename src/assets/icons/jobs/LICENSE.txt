The 24 images in this directory are NOT part of the xivcrossbar icon pack that
fills the rest of assets/icons/. They are XIVHud's own composites of the
XivParty job icon art that the party list draws:

    https://github.com/Tylas11/XivParty

XivParty ships no separate asset licence, so its source licence is the basis on
which they are redistributed here. That notice is reproduced in full below; the
same one covers the sources they are built from, in assets/xiv/jobIcons/.

What these are, and how they were made
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The party list draws a job icon as a STACK of five textures, tinted at runtime.
The crossbar cannot: a slot draws one flat picture. So each job's stack is
composited ahead of time into a single file:

    1.  assets/xiv/jobIcons/bg.png        multiplied by the job's role colour
    2.  assets/xiv/jobIcons/<job>.png     source-over
    3.  assets/xiv/jobIcons/frame.png     source-over

Two of the party list's five layers are deliberately left out, and the second
of them makes these DIFFER from what the party list draws:

    highlight.png  the party cursor, drawn only on the row the player is
                   targeting. Not part of the icon at all.
    gradient.png   a dark shading wash (44,44,44 at 40% alpha) over the
                   background. Dropped on purpose (Kevin, 2026-08-30) so the
                   background reads as one solid colour - the wash costs the
                   gold roles most of their shine at a slot's size.

The tint is a per-pixel MULTIPLY rather than a fill of bg.png's silhouette,
because that is what Windower does to a tinted prim - bg.png's edge is
antialiased white over partial alpha, and multiplying keeps that edge where a
fill would harden it.

The role colour is components/partylist/jobs.lua's role_of() resolved through
the colours in components/partylist/layout.lua, so these files encode this
addon's role table and not XivParty's. They differ in one job: RDM is a damage
dealer here, where XivParty has it on healer.

    dd       102, 53, 53     healer    59, 101, 41
    support  218, 178, 0     tank      54, 69, 151
    special  255, 151, 0

They are 64x64, the source size, and NOT resampled: a crossbar slot draws 40x40
and the party list draws these same sources at 36x36, so both sizes are reached
by scaling DOWN at draw time, which is what keeps the glyph's highlights. An
earlier pass shipped them resized to 32x32, the pack's size, and the slot then
scaled that back UP - which cost the gold roles most of their bright pixels and
was visible in a live client.

Regenerate them rather than editing a pixel: any change to the role table or to
the colours means re-running the composite for every job, and a hand-edited
file would silently disagree with the party list.

    2026-08-30  24 jobs at 32x32, RDM on dd
    2026-08-30  64x64 and no gradient - see above
