The image files in this directory and its jobIcons/ and buffIcons/
subdirectories are the "xiv" theme assets from the XivParty addon:

    https://github.com/Tylas11/XivParty

XivParty ships no separate asset licence, so its source licence is the basis on
which they are redistributed here. That notice is reproduced in full below.

The same licence covers the material ported from XivParty's source into this
component: the buff priority in buff_order.lua, the trust job table and role
map in jobs.lua, and the geometry in layout.lua.

xiv/BgTopWide.png, xiv/BgMidWide.png and xiv/BgBottomWide.png are modified
versions of XivParty's BgTop.png, BgMid.png and BgBottom.png, which are kept
here unmodified beside them as the basis for the derivation.

The originals are a horizontal gradient 377px wide: they fade in over the first
14 pixels, hold solid to x=240, then take 137 to fade away. This component's
row is wider than XivParty's, so at that width the buff icon grid and the TP
bar fell almost entirely in the falloff -- and stretching the strip scales the
falloff with it, so covering them cost a strip trailing hundreds of pixels of
fade well past the content it was supposed to end at. The Wide versions remap
the horizontal axis instead, at whatever width covers the row's content plus a
fixed ~40px falloff:

    source x   0..14    ->  out x   0..14           fade-in, unchanged
    source x  14..240   ->  out x  14..SOLID_TO      solid section, stretched
    source x 240..377   ->  out x  SOLID_TO..WIDTH   falloff, compressed

Each output column is a linear interpolation between the two source columns it
falls between. Nothing else is touched: the vertical structure, the colour and
the shape of both transitions are the originals'.

Regenerated as the row's content has grown, by rerunning the same transform at
a new SOLID_TO (never by hand-editing pixels):

    2026-08-06  SOLID_TO 410 (the row's own right edge)     WIDTH 450
    2026-08-07  SOLID_TO 502 (10 x 20px icons per row)      WIDTH 542
    2026-08-07  SOLID_TO 460 (8 x 20px icons per row)       WIDTH 500

buff_order.lua is additionally derived from Windower's generated
resources/buffs.lua, whose notice is reproduced second.
