1. THE BAR ART (xiv/), from XivParty
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    https://github.com/Tylas11/XivParty

XivParty ships no separate asset licence, so its source licence is the basis on
which these are redistributed. Three files:

    xiv/BarBG.png   the bar's background plate
    xiv/Bar.png     the fill, stretched to the health percentage
    xiv/BarFG.png   the bevelled frame drawn over both

They derive from the same files under components/partylist/assets/xiv/ (the
duplication is deliberate: a component owns everything it draws, so that
removing one cannot break another), regenerated to four times the source
width - this component draws one wide bar where the party list draws many
narrow ones. The transform preserves the art rather than resampling it:

    source cols   0..43     ->  out cols    0..43        left cap, byte for byte
    source col   128        ->  out cols   44..979       middle, replicated
    source cols 212..255    ->  out cols  980..1023      right cap, byte for byte

The middle of both frame textures is a run of identical columns in the source
(BarFG cols 44..211, BarBG wider still), so replicating the centre column
extends it without interpolating a single pixel; the vertical structure is
untouched. Bar.png is uniform across its whole width and is rebuilt the same
way at the fill region's 972 file columns.

    2026-08-08  256 -> 1024 (drawn 128 -> 512), caps 44 file px each

This notice covers only those three files - partylist's own copy carries the
additional notices for the job icons, buff icons and its regenerated
background art, none of which are here.

The same licence covers the material ported from XivParty's source into this
component: the bar geometry in logic.lua (the 128x64 frame, its 102px fill
region at a 13px inset, and the 25..39 band inside the texture) and the health
band thresholds and colours, both by way of components/partylist/layout.lua.

2. THE CLAIM STATE MACHINE, from enemybar
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    https://github.com/Windower/Lua/tree/dev/addons/enemybar

No art is taken from enemybar. What is taken is its target-colouring logic: the
six claim states, the order they are tested in, and the exact RGB values for
each - in logic.lua's `claim_state` and defaults.lua's `fill_colors`. The
reference applies that palette to its text; here it tints the bar fill instead,
and the ownership test is widened to the alliance and guarded against an
unresolved player id. The states, their order and five of the six colours are
the original's; the claimed-by-us red is deepened (255,204,204 -> 255,20,20),
because the reference's pale pink was chosen for text and reads washed out
spread across a fill.

3. THE RANGE BANDS, from DistancePlus
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

    https://github.com/Windower/Lua/tree/dev/addons/DistancePlus

No art is taken from DistancePlus either. What is taken is its range colouring:
the per-weapon band constants, the casting distances, the model-size
adjustments, the four colours and - load-bearing, since the bands overlap - the
order the branches are evaluated in, all in logic.lua's `ranged_state`,
`casting_state` and `RANGED_BANDS`. Transcribed digit for digit.
