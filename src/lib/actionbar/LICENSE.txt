Third-party notices for lib/actionbar, the engine under every action bar.

Two files here are transcribed from MIT-licensed source, and one of them from
a fork that also carries its parent's BSD notice; the notices are reproduced
in full below (MIT's condition, and BSD clause 1, both require it). Every
other file under lib/actionbar/ is XIVHud's own writing - defaults.lua
ships xivcrossbar's drawing NUMBERS, which is the tuning the port kept and
not transcribed code - or carries its upstream notice inline (render.lua:
the radial recast sweep, BSD (c) 2026 WG Incorporated).

    kebab.lua        kebab_casify, taken verbatim from xivcrossbar's
                     libs/kebab_casify.lua (MIT (c) 2020 AliekberFFXI)
    counters.lua     the stratagem charge counter from an xivcrossbar fork
                     (https://github.com/khowe085/xivcrossbar), which
                     carries its parent repository's notices: MIT (c) 2020
                     AliekberFFXI and BSD (c) 2017 SirEdeonX; plus the ninja
                     tool / master tool / Quick Draw card lookup tables and
                     the stratagem ability list, transcribed from
                     xivcrossbar's consumables.lua (MIT (c) 2020 AliekberFFXI)

Both were the crossbar component's until 2026-09-06, when the engine moved
here to be shared with the hotbar.
